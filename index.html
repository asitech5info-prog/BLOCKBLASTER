✨ What is Block Blaster?

Block Blaster is an addictive block puzzle game built with smooth tactile snapping, harmonic audio effects, and rich visuals. Clear lines, trigger explosive combo chains, and journey through 500 hand-crafted Adventure levels to win all 5 Legendary Trophies!

📖 Rules & How to Play

1
Drag or Tap to Place

Pick shapes from your bottom tray and drop them onto the 8×8 grid.

2
Blast Rows & Columns

Fill entire horizontal lines or vertical columns to vaporize blocks.

3
Chain Dynamic Combos

Blast lines on consecutive moves to escalate your combo multiplier (x2, x3...)!

4
Trigger Perfect Clears

Clear every single block off the board to activate a surprise theme change!

💡 Pro Tips

  • Keep the center clear to fit the giant 3×3 square block.
  • Clear multiple rows and columns together for mega-score blasts.
  • In Adventure Mode, target diamond blocks first to conquer the level!
