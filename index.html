✨ What is Block Blaster?

Block Blaster is an addictive block puzzle game built with tactile dragging, harmonic audio, dynamic theme transformations on perfect grid clears, and an epic 96-Level Adventure Map!

📖 Rules & How to Play

1
Drag or Tap to Place

Pick shapes from your bottom tray and drop them onto the 8×8 grid.

2
Blast Rows & Columns

Fill entire horizontal rows or vertical columns to clear blocks.

3
Chain Dynamic Combos

Blast lines on consecutive moves to multiply your score (x2, x3, x4...)!

4
Trigger Theme Transformations

Clear every block off the board to activate an instant theme switch!

💡 Pro Tips

  • Leave space for large 2×3, 3×2, and 3×3 square blocks.
  • Clear multiple rows and columns simultaneously for mega-points.
  • In Adventure Mode, achieve the target score or blast diamond blocks to win!
